FIELD NOTES01AND ONWARD

My path began with a simple kind of curiosity: how can an idea become something people can see, use, or experience?

Design gives an idea a visual voice. Programming introduces structure and logic. Artificial intelligence opens new ways to explore, while video editing adds rhythm to a message. I’m interested in the space where these disciplines meet.

As an Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Negeri Surabaya, I’m building my foundation deliberately. I don’t see learning as a checklist to finish; I see it as a practice of testing ideas, noticing what works, and improving the next attempt.
